# Bunty Biryani Centre — Vendor Quick Guide (बंटी बिरयानी सेंटर — दैनिक गाइड)

A simple one-page guide for running daily orders. Bookmark the Admin dashboard or **Add to Home screen** on your phone.

---

## 1. Morning Setup (हर सुबह 30 सेकंड में)

1. Open your admin dashboard on your phone and go to **Stock**.
2. Tap **Use yesterday's numbers** (कल वाली संख्या उपयोग करें).
3. Adjust quantities for anything prepared differently today.
4. Tap the green **Save stock** bar at the bottom.
5. Your menu is now open for customers!

---

## 2. During the Day (दिन के दौरान)

- Keep the **Today** screen open on your phone.
- When a customer places an order, your phone plays an **alert tone** and displays a badge showing new orders.
- Tap **Turn on sound** if your browser asks for permission.

---

## 3. Batch Delivery Orders (बैच डिलीवरी: MSH और कन्हार)

- **Cutoff time**: 7:30 PM (or your configured time).
- Once the slot closes, look at the Batch Card:
  - It shows **Total portions needed** (e.g., "Chicken Biryani: 14, Veg Biryani: 6, Raita: 8").
  - Pack all portions into your delivery bag.
- At the hostel gate:
  - Customers give you their Order ID (e.g. `BB1001`) and pay cash.
  - When the entire batch is delivered and collected, tap **Mark all done** (or tick off individual orders).

---

## 4. Express Delivery Orders (एक्सप्रेस डिलीवरी: 30–40 मिनट)

- Express orders appear on their own card at the top.
- They do **not** wait for batch cutoffs.
- The card shows:
  - **Customer Name & Phone Number**
  - **Hostel & Room Number**
  - **Expected delivery time** (e.g., by 8:35 PM)
- Once prepared and delivered:
  1. Collect cash → Tap **Collect Cash**.
  2. Tap **Mark Done**.

---

## 5. Emergency Controls (आपातकालीन सेटिंग्स)

All emergency buttons are on the **Settings** / **Controls** screen:

| Situation | Action |
|---|---|
| **Kitchen is overwhelmed** | Turn **Express Delivery OFF**. Customers will only see Batch orders. |
| **Out of one specific dish** | Go to **Stock** and set that item's count to `0`. It immediately shows "Sold out" to customers. |
| **Heavy rain / Stall closed / Taking a break** | Turn **Orders OFF** (Pause orders). Customers will see: *"We are currently paused. Please check back shortly."* |
| **Ready to take orders again** | Turn **Orders ON**. |

---

## 6. Fake or Cancelled Orders (फर्जी या रद्द ऑर्डर)

- If someone cancels or an order is a prank:
  1. Open the order from the list.
  2. Tap **Cancel order** and confirm.
  3. The items are **automatically added back** to your available food stock so other students can order them.

---

## 7. What If the Site Stops Working?

- If mobile network drops or the website doesn't load:
  1. Tell students on your WhatsApp groups: *"Site is down today, please send orders directly on WhatsApp."*
  2. Call developer support at **<DEVELOPER_PHONE_NUMBER>**.
